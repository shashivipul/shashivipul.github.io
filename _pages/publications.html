My research asks how machine learning systems can remain trustworthy, meaning fair, robust, privacy-aware, well-calibrated, and efficient, when they learn from complex, structured data and are deployed in the real world.

In my Ph.D. at IIT Delhi, I have pursued this through theoretical and optimization-driven methods for graph-structured and biomedical data: certifiably robust and fair graph neural networks, fairness-aware GNN-to-MLP distillation, graph coarsening, and graph–language alignment for neuroimaging. As a Research Intern at Fujitsu Research, I am carrying these questions into systems that change over time. I am developing flow-matching models that detect anomalies in dynamic graphs, and self-evolving AI agents that must keep improving without losing reliability.

Research Areas

Self-Evolving AI Agents & Dynamic Graph Anomaly Detection

At Fujitsu Research, I work on self-evolving AI agents that improve their own behaviour over time, and on flow-matching generative models for detecting anomalies in dynamic, time-evolving graphs.

Fujitsu Research · 2026–Present

Trustworthy & Efficient Graph Learning

Certifiably robust and fair GNN training under structural and feature perturbations, and fairness-aware GNN-to-MLP distillation for fast, graph-free inference. Current work examines class-conditional transfer disparities and connectivity leakage in graph-free inference.

IEEE TSPAAAITMLRDSAA

Graph Coarsening & Scalable Graph Learning

Optimization-based graph coarsening that shrinks large graphs while retaining the structural and feature information needed for downstream tasks.

Under review

Trustworthy Biomedical Representation Learning

Self-supervised and equity-aware methods for heterogeneous neuroimaging data, including multi-domain fMRI connectivity modelling for major depressive disorder and robust, fair BOLD-signal analysis under distribution shift.

MICCAIML4H

Graph–Language & Tabular LLMs

Aligning self-supervised fMRI graph representations with frozen language models for few-shot diagnosis, and evaluating tabular LLMs on healthcare and EHR data for robustness, fairness, privacy, uncertainty, and calibration.

AAAI Workshop

Deployable Healthcare AI

Deep learning for CT-based COVID-19 diagnosis in collaborative edge–cloud settings, built for efficient inference on resource-constrained hardware.

MTAP
